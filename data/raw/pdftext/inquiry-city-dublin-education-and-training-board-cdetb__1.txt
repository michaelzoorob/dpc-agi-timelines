CORRIGENDUM
DPC Inquiry Reference: IN-19-7-3
Corrigendum to the Decision of the Data Protection Commission, made on 18 June 2025 pursuant
to Section 111 of the Data Protection Act 2018 concerning City of Dublin Education and Training
Board.
1. The decision records a third party investigation having been carried out on 13 and 14 October
2018. Following the making of the decision, the data controller submitted that this investigation
was in fact carried out between 16 and 19 October 2018. To reflect this clarification, the decision
should be read as follows:
On page 6, in paragraph 21(a):
For:
‘which conducted an investigation of the SUSI website between 13 and 14 October 2018.’
Read:
‘which conducted an investigation of the SUSI website on the SUSI website between 16 and
19 October 2018.’
On page 13, in paragraph 54:
For:
‘discovered between 14 and 16 October 2018’
Read:
‘discovered on 16 October 2018’
On page 13, in paragraph 55, first bullet point:
For:
‘discovered between 14 and 16 October 2018.’
Read:
‘discovered on 16 October 2018.’
On page 14, in paragraph 55, second bullet point:
For:
‘discovered between 14 and 16 October 2018.’
Read:
‘discovered on 16 October 2018.’

On page 35, in paragraph 145:
For:
‘CDETB stated that it became aware between 14 and 16 October 2018’
Read:
‘CDETB stated that it became aware on 16 October 2018’
2. On page 68, in paragraph 283, the decision refers to a number of technical and organisational
measures implemented by the data controller in order to reduce the likelihood of similar
breaches occurring in future.
The fifth bullet point at paragraph 283 states:
‘The Grant Application System now has its own portal and is separate to the website.’
In respect of that statement, the data controller has since submitted as follows:
‘The Grant Application System has always operated through its own dedicated portal and
has been functionally and technically separate from the main website since its inception.
This separation was in place from the beginning, rather than being a recent change.’
